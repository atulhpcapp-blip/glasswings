Organiser Guide

1Create an event

Admin → Events → Create ticketed event. It's a 5-step wizard — fill each step and tap Next.
  • Basics — event title, who's hosting (Glasswings Original / Partner / Get-together), category and city. Partner organisers: add your name and logo here.
  • Media — a Poster (portrait) is what shows on the event card; a Banner (landscape) shows on the event page. Videos are optional.
  • When & where — date, start time, and always an end time (so the event auto-closes). Then Physical (add venue), Online (add link) or Venue TBD.
  • Details — description, schedule, food, dress code, line-up/artists, FAQs, terms, and entry badges (18+, Couples only, Members only…).
  • Tickets — price (0 = free), optional member discount and add-ons. Tap Create event.
Tip: New events show as Pending approval until an admin approves them. You can still edit everything in the meantime.

2Ticket types & discounts

Open your event (tap the coloured strip) → 🎟️ Tickets tab.
  • Create multiple tickets for one event — e.g. Men, Women, Couple, Early bird — each with its own price and quantity.
  • ♀ % off / ♂ % off give women or men a discount on a ticket.
  • Credits (cr) — how many in-app credits the ticket costs; leave blank for cash-only.
  • Qty caps how many of that ticket sell (blank = unlimited).
  • Tap Edit on any ticket to change the price or discount later — no need to delete and recreate.

3Guests, VIPs & Team

Open your event → 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Guest list tab. Give free entry to anyone — no account needed.

🎟️ Guest friends & plus-ones   💎 VIP sponsors, press, special guests   🛡️ Team crew, artists, security

  • Pick a tier first, then choose One (single person), Bulk (paste a whole list, one per line) or Member (search existing members).
  • Outsiders just need a name. Add a phone to send on WhatsApp, or an email to send the ticket automatically.
  • Each guest gets a unique QR ticket. Tap WhatsApp or ✉️ to send it. Change tier or add a reserved-table note anytime.
  • VIP note — add "Table 4, near stage" and it appears on their ticket, the door list and the printed sheet.
Bulk tip: paste a column straight from Excel / Google Sheets — one guest per line, optional Name, phone, email.

4The event door (check-in)

Admin → 🚪 EVENT DOOR (next to Events). This is where you admit people on the night.
  • Choose the event, then Scan tickets and point the camera at a guest's QR — green admits, red means already used or invalid.
  • No camera? Type the ticket code in the box and tap Check.
  • Door sale lets you sell a ticket on the spot and take cash or UPI.
  • The guest list is colour-coded by tier (Guest / VIP / Team) with live counts; you can also print a checklist.

5Sharing, notifying & payouts

From the event's actions and the 🖼️ Media & share tab.
  • Post pushes the event to your group chats; Notify messages ticket-holders.
  • Share & sell gives you a link and QR — anyone who opens it can buy a ticket. Download the event poster to put the QR on flyers.
  • List exports the guest/attendee list as a PDF.
  • Money from online sales is settled to the organiser; door cash/UPI is collected by you directly. See the Payouts section in Admin.
