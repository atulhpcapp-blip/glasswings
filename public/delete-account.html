Delete your Glasswings account

You can request permanent deletion of your account and associated personal data here, even if you have uninstalled the app. You do not need to sign in to view this page.

Send a deletion request

  1. Email atulhpcapp@gmail.com, preferably from the email address registered with Glasswings.
  2. Use the subject Glasswings account deletion request. Include your registered name and email address or phone number so our team can locate your account.
  3. Our team will verify ownership before processing deletion. If you cannot access your registered email, contact the same address for help verifying your account.
Email account deletion request

This button opens your email app. Review and send the email to submit your request. Opening this page or tapping the button does not delete your account. Never send your password or OTP.

If no email app opens, copy atulhpcapp@gmail.com into Gmail or your preferred email service and send the request there.

What happens to your data?

Deleting your account removes your profile and associated personal data, except records we must keep for legal or accounting reasons, such as payment records, as described in our privacy policy. Account deletion is permanent.

Bookings, payments and subscriptions

If you have an active subscription, upcoming booking, or pending refund, mention it in your request so our team can help you with it. Sending a deletion request does not itself cancel a subscription or issue a refund.

Need help?

Contact atulhpcapp@gmail.com for help with your deletion request or information about retained records.
